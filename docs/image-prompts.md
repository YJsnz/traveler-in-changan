# Image Prompts

These prompts are for early concept art and placeholder references. They should evoke an HD-2D-inspired RPG mood without copying any existing game's specific characters, UI, scenes, or assets.

## Style Board

```text
Use case: historical-scene
Asset type: style board for a Tang dynasty HD-2D-inspired RPG prototype
Primary request: a cohesive visual style board for a game called Traveler in Chang'an, showing pixel-art character readability, miniature 2.5D environment depth, lantern-lit Tang dynasty West Market props, and a restrained turn-based RPG battle UI mood
Scene/backdrop: Chang'an West Market at night after rain, tiled eaves, timber stalls, spice jars, hanging lanterns, wet stone road
Style/medium: stylized game concept art, pixel characters in a dimensional diorama-like scene, dramatic depth of field, rich lighting, painterly reference sheet composition
Lighting/mood: warm amber lanterns against cool blue-black night shadows, mysterious investigation mood
Color palette: vermilion wood, amber lantern light, weathered gray stone, aged bronze, muted jade accents, deep ink shadows
Constraints: original design, no direct copying of existing game assets, no logos, no text, no watermark
Avoid: modern objects, neon signs, plastic, generic medieval European fantasy, anime close-up portrait
```
