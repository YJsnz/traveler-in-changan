# Visual Style Guide

## Direction

Aim for a Tang dynasty HD-2D-inspired look: pixel characters in readable silhouettes, miniature stage-like environments, dramatic lighting, shallow depth, and rich material contrast.

Do not copy Octopath Traveler assets, characters, UI, or exact compositions. Use it only as a high-level reference for the blend of pixel figures, dimensional scenery, lighting, and atmosphere.

## Core Ingredients

- Pixel-art characters with limited animation frames.
- 2.5D scene composition with layered props and strong foreground/background depth.
- Warm lantern light against cool night shadows.
- Tang architectural forms: tiled eaves, timber beams, market banners, stone roads, carved doors.
- Dense but readable market dressing: spice sacks, ceramic jars, scrolls, carts, lantern stands.
- Cinematic combat UI with dark lacquer, aged bronze, muted jade, and warm paper tones.

## First Location: Chang'an West Market

Mood: late night after a festival, rain just stopped, lantern reflections on stone pavement, incense and spice stalls still glowing.

Palette:

- Lantern amber: `#d9903d`
- Vermilion wood: `#9f3028`
- Deep ink shadow: `#17191f`
- Weathered stone: `#6f7471`
- Aged bronze: `#8b6f3d`
- Muted jade accent: `#5f8a75`

## Asset Rules

- Characters should be clear at small size before they are beautiful at large size.
- Backgrounds should leave clean walkable lanes.
- Avoid modern objects, neon lighting, plastic materials, and generic fantasy armor.
- Avoid one-note red/gold palettes; mix cool night shadows and weathered stone into the scene.

## Image Generation Prompt Pattern

Use this pattern for concept art:

```text
Use case: historical-scene
Asset type: game concept art reference for a Tang dynasty HD-2D-inspired RPG
Primary request: <specific asset>
Scene/backdrop: Chang'an West Market at night, Tang dynasty architectural details, wet stone road, lanterns, spice stalls
Style/medium: stylized game concept art, pixel-art character readability mixed with miniature 2.5D scene depth, dramatic lighting, shallow depth of field
Lighting/mood: warm lantern light, cool blue-black night shadows, mysterious investigation mood
Color palette: vermilion wood, amber lanterns, weathered stone, aged bronze, muted jade accents
Constraints: original design, no direct copying of existing game assets, no logos, no text, no watermark
Avoid: modern objects, anime close-up portrait, generic medieval European fantasy, neon, plastic
```
